# Aequum Hub71 Landing Page v0.3.1

This is a positioning refinement of v0.3.

## Geographic positioning changed

The public website no longer positions Aequum specifically around Abu Dhabi.

New positioning:

**Built in India. Designed for global service businesses. Expanding next into the UAE.**

The rationale is to keep the startup identity independent of any one accelerator, city or programme while still showing a credible international expansion path.

The section now presents:

1. INDIA - Build and validate
2. UAE - First international expansion market
3. GLOBAL - Scale the engagement layer

The Hub71 application can separately explain why Abu Dhabi and Hub71 specifically are strategically relevant if Aequum is selected.

## What did not change

- Core product positioning
- Business vision
- Now / Next / Later strategy
- Business model story
- Product preview
- Early Access form
- Payload CMS
- PostgreSQL
- Admin authentication
- Security architecture
- Existing build fixes
